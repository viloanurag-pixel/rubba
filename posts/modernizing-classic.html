Platform Regulation

The future of digital law.

Published 2025-08-22

Governments are stepping in to regulate big tech. What does this mean for the average user and the platforms themselves?

Antitrust Laws

Preventing monopolies and ensuring fair competition in the digital marketplace.

Content Liability

Debates over whether platforms should be held responsible for the content posted by their users (e.g., Section 230 in the US).

Digital Services Act

New EU rules aiming to create a safer digital space where the fundamental rights of users are protected.
