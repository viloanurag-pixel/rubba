The Economics of Free Platforms

If you're not paying, you're the product.

Published 2025-08-22

Understanding the two-sided market model and how platforms monetize user attention through targeted advertising.

Two-Sided Markets

Platforms connect two distinct groups: users and advertisers (or buyers and sellers). The platform's value comes from facilitating these interactions.

Targeted Advertising

Advertisers pay to reach specific demographics. Platforms use your data to ensure ads are shown to the most relevant audience.

The Attention Economy

Your time and attention are scarce resources. Platforms compete to keep you engaged for as long as possible to show more ads.
