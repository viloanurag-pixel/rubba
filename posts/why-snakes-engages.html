How Recommendation Algorithms Work

Understanding the math that decides what you see next.

Published 2025-08-22

Algorithms are the gatekeepers of the internet. Here is how they function.

Collaborative Filtering

This method predicts what you might like based on what similar users have liked in the past.

Content-Based Filtering

This looks at the attributes of the content itself (tags, keywords) to find matches for your interests.

The Hybrid Approach

Most modern platforms use a combination of both to provide the most accurate recommendations.
